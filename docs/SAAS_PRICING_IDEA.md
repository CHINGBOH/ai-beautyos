# AI Beauty CRM SaaS Pricing Ideas

# Product Direction

AI Beauty CRM is designed for:

- Beauty salons
- Medical beauty clinics
- Wellness businesses
- Mother & baby stores
- Small service-based businesses

The system focuses on:

- customer management
- private-domain operations
- follow-up automation
- AI-assisted workflows

---

# Recommended SaaS Pricing Model

## Starter Plan

Suitable for:

- Small beauty salons
- Personal studios
- Startup wellness stores

Features:

- Customer management
- AI follow-up reminders
- Basic dashboard
- AI-generated scripts

Suggested price:

# 199 ~ 399 RMB / month

---

## Professional Plan

Suitable for:

- Medium beauty businesses
- Multi-staff operations
- Stores with private-domain traffic

Features:

- Advanced analytics
- Customer segmentation
- AI knowledge base
- Workflow automation
- Multi-user support

Suggested price:

# 699 ~ 1999 RMB / month

---

## Private Deployment

Suitable for:

- Large clinics
- Enterprise-level operations
- Custom workflow requirements

Features:

- Private deployment
- Custom AI workflows
- Dedicated support
- Custom integrations

Suggested pricing:

# 10k ~ 100k+ RMB

Depending on customization level.

---

# Business Strategy

## Do not sell AI.

Sell:

- customer retention
- operational efficiency
- follow-up automation
- repurchase improvement
- staff workflow optimization

---

# Recommended First Step

Do not scale immediately.

First:

- test with 3~5 stores
- collect real feedback
- improve workflows
- build real cases

Then:

- standardize the product
- optimize onboarding
- improve SaaS experience
