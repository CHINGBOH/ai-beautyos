# AI Beauty CRM Demo Script

## Demo Goal

Show beauty salons, wellness stores, and medical beauty clinics how AI Beauty CRM can:

- Reduce customer loss
- Improve follow-up efficiency
- Improve repurchase rates
- Simplify private-domain operations

---

# Demo Flow

## 1. Customer Registration

Show:

- New customer creation
- Basic customer profile
- Service history

Explain:

Traditional stores usually manage this manually.

AI Beauty CRM centralizes all customer data.

---

## 2. AI Customer Tagging

Show:

- AI-generated customer labels
- Customer segmentation
- Lifecycle analysis

Explain:

AI automatically helps stores identify customer value and behavior.

---

## 3. Follow-up Automation

Show:

- Follow-up reminders
- Silent customer alerts
- Scheduled follow-up workflow

Explain:

The system reduces missed follow-ups and improves retention.

---

## 4. AI Follow-up Script Generation

Show:

- AI-generated customer messages
- Follow-up suggestions
- Personalized communication

Explain:

Staff can save time and improve communication quality.

---

## 5. Dashboard & Analytics

Show:

- Customer statistics
- Conversion funnel
- Operational analytics
- Store overview dashboard

Explain:

Managers can understand store operations more clearly.

---

# Suggested Demo Length

3 to 5 minutes.

Keep it simple.

Focus on:

- Real business scenarios
- Customer management
- Automation
- Store operations

Avoid over-explaining technical architecture.

---

# Suggested Demo Ending

AI Beauty CRM is designed for real-world beauty and wellness businesses.

The goal is not just AI.

The goal is helping stores:

- save time
- improve customer retention
- improve operational efficiency
- build better private-domain operations
