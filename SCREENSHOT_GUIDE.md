# AI Beauty CRM Screenshot Guide

# Why Screenshots Matter

For AI SaaS products:

# Screenshots are more important than code.

Business users:

- do not understand architecture
- do not care about orchestration
- do not read complex technical documentation

They want to see:

- clean UI
- dashboards
- customer management
- automation workflows
- operational visibility

---

# Recommended Screenshots

## 1. Login Page

Goal:

Show product professionalism.

Recommended style:

- clean
- minimal
- SaaS-like

---

## 2. Dashboard

Must show:

- customer statistics
- conversion metrics
- operational overview
- charts and analytics

This is one of the most important screenshots.

---

## 3. Customer Management Page

Show:

- customer tags
- lifecycle stages
- follow-up status
- interaction history

This creates strong business credibility.

---

## 4. AI Analysis Interface

Show:

- AI recommendations
- AI-generated scripts
- customer insights
- automation suggestions

This demonstrates the "AI" value clearly.

---

## 5. Workflow / Automation

Show:

- follow-up automation
- reminder workflows
- AI operation flow

Even simple flow diagrams work.

---

## 6. Mobile UI

Very important.

Most beauty/wellness businesses are mobile-first.

Recommended screenshots:

- customer follow-up
- notifications
- dashboards
- quick operations

---

# Recommended Visual Style

## Good Direction

- white background
- soft colors
- simple cards
- clean typography
- dashboard feeling

---

## Avoid

- terminal screenshots
- raw logs
- complex engineering diagrams
- developer-heavy visuals
- dark hacker-style presentation

---

# Demo Video Suggestions

Length:

# 3~5 minutes

Flow:

1. Login
2. Dashboard
3. Customer registration
4. AI customer analysis
5. AI follow-up generation
6. Data analytics

Focus on:

- business workflows
- automation
- customer management

Avoid deep technical explanations.

---

# Final Goal

The repository should visually feel like:

# a real SaaS business system

instead of:

# a developer demo project.
