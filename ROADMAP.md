# AI Beauty CRM Roadmap

# Vision

Build an AI-powered operating system for beauty, wellness, and private-domain businesses.

The goal is not just software.

The goal is helping stores:

- improve customer retention
- improve repurchase rates
- automate follow-up workflows
- reduce manual operations
- build long-term private-domain management
